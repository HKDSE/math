AR Marker Guide

This application utilizes AR.js 3x3 Matrix Barcodes to track planets in real space.

You can print these markers or display them on a secondary device (like a phone or tablet).

  • Barcode 0 - Mercury
  • Barcode 1 - Venus
  • Barcode 2 - Earth
  • Barcode 3 - Mars
  • Barcode 4 - Jupiter
  • Barcode 5 - Saturn
  • Barcode 6 - Uranus
  • Barcode 7 - Neptune

🔍 Tip: You can scan these directly from this screen with another device.
